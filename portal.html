PART 6: EU Artificial Intelligence Act Compliance

Business Reform designs and operates the Service in line with Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 laying down harmonised rules on artificial intelligence (the "EU AI Act"), as its obligations become applicable, alongside the GDPR and the other laws referred to in these Terms.

6.1 Role of the Company and AI Systems Used

The AI CFO is an AI system operated by Business Reform under its own name. It is built on general-purpose AI models supplied by third-party AI model providers (see Section 5.4), which are subject to their own obligations as providers of general-purpose AI models under Chapter V of the EU AI Act. Business Reform acts as the provider of the AI CFO system as offered to Users and as a deployer of the underlying models.

6.2 Risk Classification and Intended Purpose

The intended purpose of the AI CFO is to transform business financial and operational data into financial analysis, reports, KPI dashboards and projections for companies. The Service:

  • Does not engage in any prohibited AI practice under Article 5 of the EU AI Act
  • Is not intended for any high-risk use listed in Annex III of the EU AI Act, including evaluating the creditworthiness of natural persons or establishing their credit score, risk assessment and pricing for life or health insurance, or decisions on employment, recruitment or access to essential services
  • Is therefore treated as an AI system subject to the transparency obligations of Article 50 of the EU AI Act

Users must not use the Service or its AI Outputs for a high-risk purpose listed in Annex III, or for any purpose prohibited by Article 5. A User who does so acts outside the intended purpose of the Service and assumes the corresponding obligations under the EU AI Act.

6.3 Transparency and Marking of AI-Generated Content

In accordance with Article 50 of the EU AI Act:

  • Users are informed, before and during use, that they are interacting with an AI system, including when using the chat assistant
  • Every Report generated by the Service carries a visible "AI generated" mark with a reference to the EU AI Act: at the bottom left of every slide in PowerPoint presentations, and on the cover sheet and at the bottom left of every printed page in Excel workbooks
  • Generated files also carry a machine-readable statement in their document metadata identifying the content as AI-generated

Users must not remove, conceal or alter the AI-generated marking when sharing Reports with third parties. Where a User publishes AI Outputs to inform the public on matters of public interest, the User is responsible for any disclosure required of it under Article 50(4), unless the content has undergone human review or editorial control and a person holds editorial responsibility for its publication.

6.4 Human Oversight

The Service is a decision-support tool. AI Outputs are designed to be reviewed by the User and, where appropriate, by qualified professionals before they are relied upon (see Part 4). No decision producing legal effects on, or similarly significantly affecting, a natural person is taken by the Service solely through automated processing. Users may request review of a Report by a member of the Business Reform team.

6.5 Accuracy, Traceability and Record-Keeping

To support accuracy and accountability, the Service:

  • Computes key figures with deterministic calculations and presents them as live Excel formulas linked to the underlying data, so they can be checked

These measures reduce but do not eliminate the risk of errors. The disclaimers in Part 4 continue to apply.
